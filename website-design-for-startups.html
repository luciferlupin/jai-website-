Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a startup website?

A basic marketing website for a startup: 5–7 business days. A landing page with waitlist or lead capture: 2–3 days. An MVP web application: 3–6 weeks depending on feature scope. We provide a working prototype within 48 hours of the discovery call so you can see progress immediately.

What does a startup website cost at Curious Kaizer?

Startup marketing websites start from ₹15,200. MVP web applications start from ₹33,900. SaaS platform development starts from ₹75,000. We provide fixed-price quotes — no hourly billing. International pricing is available in USD for US, UAE, and UK-based startups.

Can you build an MVP that is investor-ready?

Yes. We've built investor-facing products and demos. We know what investors look for: a working core feature, clean UI, responsive design, basic analytics, and a clear product narrative. We build MVPs that can be demonstrated live in due diligence meetings.

Do you do ongoing development after the MVP?

Yes. Many startups stay with Curious Kaizer for ongoing development as they grow. We can work on a monthly retainer basis or per-sprint fixed-price contracts. We hand over full source code at the end of every sprint so you are never locked in.
