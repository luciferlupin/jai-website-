FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Why should I choose Headless WordPress over traditional WordPress?

Traditional WordPress relies on heavy PHP themes and dozens of plugins that slow down mobile load times and create security vulnerabilities. Headless WordPress uses WordPress solely as a content backend while serving your pages via ultra-fast React/Edge frontend for 10x speed.

Can you fix my existing slow WordPress website?

Yes! We can audit, clean up, and rebuild your slow WordPress website into a sub-second modern web app while preserving all your existing blog posts, URLs, and SEO rankings.
