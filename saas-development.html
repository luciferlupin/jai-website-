SaaS Product Development

A SaaS product combines a repeatable customer workflow with the infrastructure needed to operate it for multiple organizations. Curious Kaizer scopes SaaS applications from the first useful customer journey through tenant access, billing integration, observability and handover.

Find the smallest useful release

An MVP should let a defined customer complete a valuable task and understand the result. Separate essential workflow steps from reporting, customization and administrator conveniences. Document the assumptions a pilot needs to test, including onboarding effort and willingness to use the product. Avoid building a large settings system before there is evidence that customers need those choices.

Tenant boundaries and roles

Define whether a user can belong to several organizations and how invitations, suspension and ownership transfer work. Enforce organization boundaries on every server-side query, export, file and background job. UI filtering alone is insufficient. Test two tenants with overlapping record identifiers and deliberately attempt cross-tenant access. Administrative support access should be explicit and auditable.

Subscription and entitlement design

A billing provider processes payments; your application still needs authoritative entitlement rules. Decide what happens during a trial, failed renewal, upgrade, refund and cancellation. Process webhook events idempotently and reconcile them against provider records. Keep features tied to entitlements rather than scattered checks on plan names. Confirm provider availability and fees for the operating business before committing to an integration.

Operating the application

Plan logs that help diagnose failures without exposing customer content. Monitor errors, queue delays and resource limits. Define backup restoration and account deletion procedures and test them with sample data. A support team needs enough context to solve problems without unrestricted access to every tenant. Rate limits and usage budgets matter when a feature calls a paid external API.

Pilot, measure and extend

Invite a small agreed pilot group and collect consent-appropriate product feedback. Measure successful task completion and failure points before adding feature breadth. Acceptance includes tenant isolation, billing event replay, invitation expiry and restore checks. A SaaS quotation should distinguish initial build, hosting, paid services and ongoing operational responsibilities. Bring a user journey and pricing hypothesis to the discovery discussion.
