Hospitality Websites and Operations Tools

Hospitality journeys connect guests, reservations and staff operations. A hotel website and an internal property dashboard solve different problems but need a reliable handoff between enquiry, availability and service delivery.

Discuss your projectExplore selected work

Separate enquiry from confirmed booking

Define whether the site takes enquiries, sends visitors to a booking engine or confirms reservations itself. A contact form does not establish room availability. Display only approved room descriptions, policies and offers. Where a booking provider handles confirmation and payment, test the return path and make responsibility clear to the guest.

Operational workflows

Map room status, arrivals, departures, housekeeping and maintenance as distinct tasks. A room marked clean is not necessarily ready for occupancy if maintenance is outstanding. Staff need concise queues and role-specific access. Record changes and exceptions so a shift handover does not depend on memory or an unstructured messaging thread.

Integration and information boundaries

Identify the property-management or booking system that owns inventory and reservation state. Avoid two systems independently accepting the last available room. If synchronization fails, show uncertainty and route work to staff rather than fabricating availability. Restrict access to guest documents and contact details, and define retention with the operator.

Proof and acceptance

The portfolio includes hospitality interface work that can anchor a discussion of staff tools. It is not evidence of occupancy gains or a live connection to every booking vendor. Bring current forms, role definitions and booking-provider documentation. Test duplicate reservations, date changes, time-zone boundaries and staff access removal before release. The project scope should name who operates integrations and handles incidents.
