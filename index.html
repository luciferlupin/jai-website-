FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

I want to make a website — where do I start?

Starting is easy! You don't need any technical background. Simply request a free prototype or contact us via WhatsApp (+91 85951 21436). We will analyze your business niche, recommend the best structure (e-commerce, corporate, clinic, or custom web app), and deliver an interactive prototype within 48 hours.

How much does a 5-page business website cost in Delhi?

A professional 5-page custom business website in Delhi starts at ₹15,200. This complete package includes: custom UI/UX design, mobile responsiveness, fast edge hosting setup, SSL certificate, contact & WhatsApp lead forms, Google Maps integration, and comprehensive on-page SEO. For online stores, packages start at ₹21,900, with 100% transparent pricing and no hidden costs.

What is the difference between static and dynamic website design?

Static websites deliver pre-rendered HTML/CSS directly from global CDN edge servers with unbeatable sub-50ms speed, maximum security, and zero database maintenance — ideal for portfolios, corporate brochures, and landing pages. Dynamic websites (such as e-commerce stores, real estate portals, and SaaS CRMs) connect to live databases (Supabase, PostgreSQL, APIs) to process live inventory, user logins, interactive calculators, and payment transactions in real time.

Do you provide free domain, SSL, and business email hosting?

Yes, absolutely! All our comprehensive packages include: automatic HTTPS SSL security certificates, custom domain DNS configuration (e.g. yourbrand.com), and business email routing (e.g. info@yourbrand.com). We configure everything on high-performance global edge cloud networks (Vercel, Cloudflare, Netlify) so your site never goes down.

How long does it take to make a complete e-commerce website?

Our rapid agile workflow delivers working prototypes in 48 hours and complete production-ready e-commerce websites in 7 to 10 days. This includes product catalog uploads, Razorpay/UPI/Stripe checkout integrations, automated WhatsApp invoice notifications, and end-to-end mobile checkout testing.

Will my website rank on Google for local searches in Delhi NCR?

Yes! Every website we build includes advanced technical SEO: Google Search Console verification, dynamic XML sitemaps, structured JSON-LD Schema (LocalBusiness, Organization, FAQPage, AggregateRating), OpenGraph social metadata, and sub-50ms Core Web Vitals optimization to help you rank in the top Google organic results and the Google Maps Local 3-Pack.

Do I get 100% full source code ownership and admin control?

100% full ownership — zero vendor lock-in! You receive the complete Git repository, full Intellectual Property (IP) rights, hosting credentials, and admin database access upon delivery. You are never held hostage by proprietary monthly builder lock-ins.

Can you redesign my existing slow WordPress or Wix website?

Yes, we specialize in high-performance migrations! We will preserve your existing URL rankings, migrate all your content and customer records, and rebuild your frontend into a sleek, blazing-fast web application with modern aesthetics and zero plugin bloat.
