FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

I want to make a website — where do I start?

Starting is easy! Contact us for a free consultation. We'll discuss your business needs, target audience, and goals. Then we'll provide a detailed proposal with timeline and cost. The entire process takes 2–4 weeks from start to launch.

What is the cost of website making in Delhi?

Website making costs in Delhi vary from ₹15,200 to ₹50,000+ depending on complexity. Basic business websites start at ₹15,200, e-commerce sites at ₹21,900, and custom web applications at ₹33,900+. We offer transparent pricing with no hidden charges.

How long does it take to make a website?

Simple websites take 7–10 days, business websites 2–3 weeks, and complex e-commerce sites 4–6 weeks. We provide exact timelines in our proposal and keep you updated throughout the development process.

Can you make a website for my small business?

Absolutely! We specialize in websites for small businesses. We understand your budget constraints and create professional websites that help you compete with larger companies. Starting at just ₹15,200, it's an investment that pays for itself.

Do I need technical knowledge to make a website?

No technical knowledge required! We handle everything from design to development to hosting. You just need to provide your business information and preferences. We also provide training so you can easily update your website later.
