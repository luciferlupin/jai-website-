Real Problems Real Estate Businesses Face

We've studied and solved these problems — not just read about them.

❌ Lead Response Too Slow

Meta Ad leads sit in a spreadsheet for hours before any agent sees them. By then, the prospect has already contacted a competitor.

❌ No Single Source of Truth

Leads in WhatsApp, deals in Excel, project updates in email, site visits in a diary. Five systems, zero visibility into pipeline health.

❌ Agent Accountability Gap

Management has no real-time visibility into which agents are following up, converting leads, or hitting targets. Coaching is reactive, not proactive.

❌ Manual Reporting to Investors

Investor update reports are manually compiled monthly, taking days. Investors have no live view of project status, bookings, or revenue.

❌ Website That Doesn't Convert

A brochure website with no lead capture, no WhatsApp integration, no site visit booking form. Traffic arrives and leaves without converting.

❌ Site Visit Chaos

Site visits scheduled verbally or on WhatsApp, with no system to track attendance, feedback, or follow-up actions. Opportunities lost.

How the Meta Lead Pipeline Works

The moment a prospect fills your Meta Ads form, this is what happens.

Meta Lead Form Submit

Prospect fills lead form on Facebook or Instagram ad. Name, phone, property interest captured.

Webhook → CRM

Meta Lead Gen webhook fires instantly. Lead appears in your CRM in under 3 seconds. Zero manual data entry.

Auto WhatsApp Message

Official WhatsApp Business API sends a personalised message to the lead within 60 seconds of form submission.

Agent Assignment

CRM assigns lead to the next available agent based on rotation rules. Agent gets push notification with full lead context.

Deal Pipeline Entry

Lead enters Kanban pipeline at "New Lead" stage. Agent moves it through stages: Contact Made → Site Visit → Negotiation → Booking.

Follow-up Reminders

Automated reminders sent to agent at scheduled intervals. Management dashboard shows overdue follow-ups in red.
