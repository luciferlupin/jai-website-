About Curious Kaizer

Turning Business Problems into Software, AI & Scalable Products.

We are a high-velocity software engineering studio based in Delhi, India. We eliminate bloat and compress traditional months of software development into 7–14 day rapid delivery sprints — with zero compromises on type safety, speed, or scalability.

Our Engineering Philosophy

Most software agencies overcomplicate architectures, relying on sluggish templates and heavy abstraction layers that slow down your business and drive up ongoing server bills.

At Curious Kaizer, we craft clean, high-performance systems. By employing ultra-lightweight Vanilla JS architectures, Three.js 3D web visualizers, serverless PostgreSQL database branching, and context-engineered AI agent pipelines, we deliver sub-50ms INP speeds and instant time-to-market.

Leadership & Founder

Founded and led by Jai Goel (@luciferlupin), a Delhi-based systems architect and full-stack software engineer.

Jai works directly with founders and enterprise executives to translate complex operational bottlenecks into sleek, responsive web platforms, AI workflow agents, and bespoke business dashboards that generate real ROI.
